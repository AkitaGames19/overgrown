How to play
  1. Walk with the arrow keys or WASD. Every square you step on grows a hedge overnight.
  2. Sleep (Space) ends the day early. Each morning you start again from your doormat.
  3. The fox takes a step every time you do. If it catches you, the day ends, but your trail still grows.
  4. Foxes can't step on your doormat. Plan your hedges to block the fox and make a way out through the garden gate.
